# Privacy Policy for Sudoku

**Last Updated:** September 12, 2026

**Sudoku** does not collect, track, transmit, or share any personal information.

---

### 1. No Data Collection
We do not collect any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage statistics. The app contains no third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertisements.

### 2. Local Storage Only
Your game progress, puzzle state, and statistics are stored exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you clear app data or uninstall the app.

### 3. Children's Privacy
The app collects no information from anyone, including children, and complies with COPPA and global privacy standards.
